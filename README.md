# the-green-screen
The Green Screen is a digital magazine and archive for climate fiction about the built environment. It publishes short stories, essays, and poetry about buildings (think cinemas, libraries, schools, housing blocks) and pairs each piece with a real building and a real retrofit solution. The project has three parts. The Archive is the magazine itself, an open-submission platform for writing about how the places we live in shape us. The Atlas is a companion database of buildings and retrofit interventions, cross-referenced with the stories. The Screening Room is a series of recorded readings and conversations that turn the archive into a community. The premise is simple: green retrofitting is usually framed as an engineering problem, but the people who inhabit buildings experience it as a story. The Green Screen gives that story a place to be told.
